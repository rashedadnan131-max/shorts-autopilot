Shorts Autopilot

A personal publishing tool

Shorts Autopilot is a private tool used by a single creator to upload short videos that they made themselves to their own YouTube channels. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

When it runs, it uploads a finished video file from the creator's own computer to the creator's own YouTube channel through the official YouTube Data API, with the title, description and tags the creator prepared. It does not read, collect or change anything else in the account.
